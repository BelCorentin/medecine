Castle Meditation Retreat

A Journey Beyond the Ordinary

Embark on a transformative spiritual journey in a majestic castle nestled in the heart of Southwest France. Experience the profound connection between meditation and consciousness expansion in a setting of timeless beauty and tranquility.

✨ The Sacred Venue ✨

Château de Lumière

Our retreat takes place in a stunning 17th-century castle in the Occitanie region of Southwest France. Surrounded by rolling vineyards and ancient forests, this historic château offers the perfect sanctuary for inner exploration.

Location: Nestled between Toulouse and Bordeaux, where the Pyrenees mountains meet the Atlantic horizon. The castle features 20 private suites, meditation halls with vaulted ceilings, sacred gardens, and panoramic terraces overlooking 50 acres of pristine nature.

🌀 The Experience 🌀

Guided Meditation

Daily meditation sessions led by experienced practitioners from various traditions including Vipassana, Transcendental, and Kundalini. Learn ancient techniques to quiet the mind and expand consciousness.

Sacred Ceremonies

Participate in traditional ceremonial experiences facilitated by trained guides. Journey into the depths of your psyche in a safe, supported environment designed for transformation and healing.

Nature Integration

Connect with the ancient energy of the land through forest bathing, sunrise yoga, and contemplative walks through lavender fields and oak groves that have stood for centuries.

Holistic Wellness

Nourish your body with organic, locally-sourced vegetarian cuisine. Enjoy sound healing sessions, breathwork classes, and therapeutic bodywork from skilled practitioners.

Creative Expression

Explore your inner landscape through art therapy, ecstatic dance, and music. Express the ineffable through various creative modalities in our dedicated art studio.

Integration Circles

Share your journey with fellow travelers in supportive group settings. Process experiences and insights with compassionate facilitators trained in transpersonal psychology.

🔮 Essential Information 🔮

Duration

Our retreats run for 7 days (6 nights), providing ample time for deep transformation while maintaining a sustainable pace for integration.

Group Size

Limited to 20 participants to ensure intimate group dynamics and personalized attention from facilitators. Early registration is recommended.

What to Bring

Comfortable meditation cushion, journal, weather-appropriate clothing, swimwear for the hot springs, and an open heart. All other amenities provided.

Preparation

We recommend a simple diet and meditation practice for two weeks prior. Detailed preparation guidelines will be sent upon registration.
